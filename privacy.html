TrackWeave: Train Yard Puzzle

TrackWeave saves chapter progress, stars, daily challenge completions, revealed hints, and preferences on your device. The game has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or developer collection of gameplay data.

You can erase the saved game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you choose Send feedback, TrackWeave opens your email app with the recipient and subject filled in. If you send the message, the developer receives your email address and the information you include. Your email provider handles the message.

For privacy questions, contact the developer at hukaimiao@gmail.com.
